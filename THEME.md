## 🎛️ Synthesis & Performance Styling

In **ShallotWHAM**, the Shallot Plum theme maps directly into the performance hardware console:
- **Lead Synth Engine**: Velvet Plum matrix with warm copper dials, LED displays (`#d48244`), and soft lilac typography.
- **Bassline Engine**: Deep Plum chassis with radiant golden amber dials (`#f39c12`) and high-impact indicators.
- **Side Wood Panels**: Royal plum wood cheeks (`#5a2e76`) for an authentic boutique analog synthesizer aesthetic.
- **Oscilloscope**: Real-time waveform visualizer dynamically shifts from cyber cyan to vibrant copper glow.
- **Keybed**: Plum key caps with copper and gold activation backlights.
